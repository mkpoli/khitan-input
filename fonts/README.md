# Fonts

- `QinglingSeal-Regular.ttf`, `.woff2`: Qingling Seal, a 16-glyph pilot of the Xuanyi seal-script
  cover at U+E700–U+E70F. The glyphs are traced from the CC BY-SA 4.0 photograph of the stone and
  offered under CC BY-SA 4.0; the 30-unit round pen, 1024-unit em and fixed advance follow Sealie
  (SIL OFL 1.1). The glyphs are a draft and need hand correction. See
  [data/seal/font/README.md](../data/seal/font/README.md).
